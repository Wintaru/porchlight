I finally finished the cedar planter box, and the raccoons approve

@marisol · Sep 9, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

Three weekends, one trip back to the lumber yard, and a lot of sanding. I wanted something that would sit on the back porch, hold a season of tomatoes, and not fall apart when the sprinkler hits it.

Cedar was the right call. It is soft enough to cut with a hand saw and it smells like a sauna for a week. The corners are simple butt joints with exterior screws, and I lined the inside with landscape fabric so the soil stays put.

What I would change: make it two inches shorter so it fits under the railing, and pre-drill everything. I split one board on the last screw and had to start that side over.
